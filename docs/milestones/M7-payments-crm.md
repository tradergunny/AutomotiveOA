# M7 — Payments & CRM

The money-and-relationships milestone. Goal: the shop's own money and relationships become workable lists — M4 priced and authorized the work, M5 made the day visible, M6 gave the customer their half of the story; M7 records what was actually received, shows what is still owed, and turns declined work into a list someone can chase. Payments land against cases (including delivered ones — insurers pay weeks late), the board gains its final **Balance due** group, Follow-ups become real rows staff work by hand, and the Customer/Vehicle history split lands as two views. No Claim records, no tax documents, no reporting; the shop now knows *who owes what, and who to call*.

**Branch:** `m7-payments-crm` · **Gate:** money owed and follow-ups are workable lists.

## Scope (in)
1. **Schema — Payment, FollowUp, plus CaseEvent additions.**
   - **Payment**: RepairCase FK (same-shop composite), `amountSatang` (positive `Int`, lib/money.ts bounds), `method` (**`CASH / TRANSFER / CARD`** — CONTEXT.md's fixed set; PromptPay is a transfer), `payerType` (`CUSTOMER / INSURER`) with `customerId` (same-shop composite FK, set on CUSTOMER rows — the person credited with the spending, decision 6) or free-text `insurerName` (mirrors `Job.insurerName`; the Claim FK arrives in M8), `receivedAt` (date-pickable, defaults today — money is often entered after it arrived), optional note, recording Staff + `recordedAt`. **Append-only with a one-way void** (decision 3): no update path for any substantive field; `voidedAt` / `voidedByStaffId` / `voidReason` settable once, Manager-only, never clearable.
   - **FollowUp** (decision 4): RepairCase FK (same-shop composite), `customerId` (the case's contact at delivery — relationship data), exactly one source link (nullable `jobId` / `findingId`, same-case composite FKs — safe, since delivered cases never delete either), snapshot `title` + nullable `quotedPriceSatang` (the "quoted ฿18,000 in March" the chase leans on), `status` (**`OPEN / SNOOZED / CONTACTED / DROPPED`**), nullable `snoozedUntil`, last-action Staff / at / note, `createdAt`. One FollowUp per source (`@@unique` per source column; NULLs-distinct idiom) so the delivery mint and the backfill are idempotent.
   - **CaseEventType** gains `PAYMENT_RECORDED`, `PAYMENT_VOIDED`, `FOLLOW_UP_CONTACTED`, `FOLLOW_UP_SNOOZED`, `FOLLOW_UP_DROPPED`, `FOLLOW_UP_REOPENED` (additive migration, values unused in the same transaction — the M6 idiom). CaseEvent gains nullable `paymentId` and `followUpId` (real composite FKs — neither row type is ever deleted); the money an event carries rides the existing `priceSatang` column with its doc comment generalized. No `FOLLOW_UP_CREATED` events — the mint is part of `CASE_DELIVERED` and the row's `createdAt` records it.
   - All classified in `lib/tenant.ts`; tenant-guard tests extended to both models and every new link.
2. **Balance derivation** — one shared helper (lib/money.ts's sibling): per payer side, **owed = Σ price of that side's currently-authorized-or-beyond Jobs (`AUTHORIZED / WAITING / IN_PROGRESS / QC / COMPLETED`) − Σ that side's non-voided Payments**; blended balance = the sum of both sides. `DECLINED` never owes; `CANCELLED` deliberately owes nothing in MVP (billing partially-done stopped work is real but messy — interview checklist). Every counted Job has a price by construction (M4: authorization requires one). Case header, board, and customer page all call this helper — the number is never re-derived ad hoc.
3. **Payment recording on the case page** — the money summary grows into a real money section: the split balance (decision 2), the payment list (amount, method, payer, received date, recorded-by, note; voided rows struck through with who/why), **Record payment** (baht input via lib/money.ts, method, payer side defaulted to CUSTOMER, insurer name prefilled from the case's insurer Jobs, received date, note) — **allowed on DELIVERED cases** (decision 5's re-scoped guard), and Manager-only **Void** with a required reason. Recording and voiding each write their CaseEvent in the same transaction; a deposit is just an early Payment (CONTEXT.md), no separate concept.
4. **Board — Balance due group, and the pickup amount** (decision 1) — the trailing **Balance due** group D-2 reserved in M5: **delivered cases with blended balance > 0**. These rows render for money, not work: reference, plate + vehicle, contact, the split balance (D-3 red on the amount), days since delivered. Open cases never file here — first-match precedence keeps them in their work group; **Ready for pickup** rows instead gain the amount to collect at handover. A case leaves the board when its balance clears and returns if a void resurrects the debt. The group's query aggregates Jobs and Payments across delivered cases — cheap at garage scale; a materialized balance column is a later optimization if it ever isn't.
5. **Follow-up worklist** (`/followups` — the stub becomes real). Default view = actionable (OPEN plus SNOOZED whose `snoozedUntil` has passed — derived on read, no cron); filters for the other states. Rows: customer + phone, plate, source ("windshield replacement — quoted ฿18,000" or "brake pads — needs work"), case reference, age since delivery, LINE-link state chip, snooze date where set. Actions: **open** (→ the composer, §6), **mark contacted** (optional note — the phone path), **snooze** (until a date), **drop** (required reason), **reopen**. Every transition updates the row and writes its typed CaseEvent on the source case.
6. **Follow-up → composer** (decision 5) — the M6 composer **un-disables on Delivered cases** (M6's read-only line is superseded); opening a follow-up deep-links to the source case's composer pre-filled with a **follow-up-specific Thai-first draft** (names the declined work and its quoted price, or the wear item — customer-safe wording, fully editable; a second template in lib/line-draft.ts, Thai-first data per the M6 precedent). A successful send launched from a follow-up marks it CONTACTED in the same recording transaction — bookkeeping caused by a human's send, the ADR-003-safe direction — with the CaseEvent linking follow-up and Update. All M6 disable-states (not linked, unfollowed) behave unchanged; the worklist's phone number and LINE chip let staff pick the channel before opening anything.
7. **Customer history view** (`/customers/[id]` extended) — relationship data (decision 6): **spending** (Σ of their own non-voided CUSTOMER Payments, all cases), visits (cases where they were the contact, with status and date), their payment rows, their Follow-ups (actionable and past), vehicles they are primary for, and the M6 LINE-link state. Insurer receipts show in visit context but never move the spending number.
8. **Vehicle history view** (new `/vehicles/[id]` — only edit exists today) — physical truth (decision 6): identity header (plate, make/model, body type, current primary Customer), then every Repair Case chronologically **across owners**: work done (completed Jobs), parts replaced, photos. **No money anywhere on this view** — prices and Payments are relationship data and stay with the person. Reached from case pages, customer pages, and check-in; no vehicle index page in MVP.
9. **Docs, seed, backfill** — DESIGN.md D-2 amended with decision 1's board rule; CONTEXT.md's Repair Case paragraph gains the re-scoped delivery freeze (decision 5) and the Follow-up paragraph the delivery mint (decision 4). Seed stays idempotent and seeds **no** Payments or FollowUps — performing the flow is the gate. A small idempotent `npm run followups:backfill` mints FollowUps for pre-M7 delivered cases (decision 4 — unlike M5's no-backfill, this materializes present state from frozen data, not fabricated history).

All new UI bilingual through the M1 plumbing — payment methods, void dialog, balance and board labels, follow-up states and actions, event labels, validation, empty states in both message files. The follow-up draft template is Thai-first data, never translated to the staff locale. Money renders through `formatBaht` with tabular numerals everywhere it columns up.

## Scope (out — do not build)
- **Claim records** (M8) — `Payment.insurerName` free text mirrors Job's until then. **Tax invoices / receipts (ใบกำกับภาษี) and VAT** ([LATER.md](../LATER.md)) — M7 records money, it produces no customer-facing money document of any kind.
- **Money out**: refunds, write-offs, deposits-returned. A bad debt sits visible in Balance due — deliberately (interview checklist: do garages need write-off?).
- **Payment terms / due dates / "overdue" math** — days-since-delivered is the only aging signal in MVP.
- **Per-Job payment allocation** — a Payment lands in its payer's bucket on the case, nothing finer (decision 2).
- **Anything that sends or nags automatically** — payment reminders, snooze pings, insurer-overdue notifications (ADR-003). Dashboard update-nudges stay in [LATER.md](../LATER.md).
- **Case-less customer-level LINE Updates** — `LineUpdate.caseId` stays required; the M6 seam stays closed (decision 5).
- **Cancelled-Job follow-ups** — CONTEXT.md sources the list from Declined Jobs and never-authorized wear Findings; a Cancelled Job became authorized work. Noted for the pilot (decision 4).
- **Reporting/analytics** (revenue by month, top customers, technician utilization) — the spending number on the customer page is the entire CRM aggregate in MVP. **Fleet/corporate billing** ([LATER.md](../LATER.md)).
- **Case-page density pass, bilingual copy pass, print styles, second-Shop dry-run, backups, production deploy guide** — all M8.

## Decisions — ruled by the founder 2026-08-27
All six approved as recommended.

- **Balance due vs. the board — approved: CONTEXT.md holds verbatim; M5's "delivered cases leave the board entirely" bends to "delivered cases leave the board once nothing is owed."** The two rules cannot coexist: the cases with money outstanding are mostly delivered ones (insurers pay weeks late — that is CONTEXT.md's own reasoning), so a Balance due group on an open-cases-only board would be near-empty and useless. The board invariant becomes: **every open case exactly once, plus every delivered case still owed money, in the trailing Balance due group, until its balance clears.** What it costs: the board stops being a map of cars in the lot — Balance due rows are cars already gone, so they render for money, not work (no job rollup, no technicians); a case can *return* to the board (a void resurrects the debt); and the group grows unboundedly if debts never clear — accepted, because that visibility is the feature, and there is no write-off in MVP. Open cases with money outstanding never file here — precedence keeps them in their work group, and Ready rows show the amount to collect at pickup instead. (Rejected: keeping the board open-only with a separate outstanding-balances page — preserves M5's sentence but breaks CONTEXT.md's, and moves the debts most worth chasing onto a second screen nobody opens, violating M5's own "the shop's day is visible on one screen".) D-2 gets the one-line amendment, as M5 amended it for In assessment.
- **One balance or two — approved: two, derived per payer side, everywhere money shows.** A case carries Customer-pays and Insurer-pays Jobs at once and the payers settle weeks apart, so one blended number answers no question anyone at the counter actually asks ("what do I collect at pickup?" and "what is the insurer still sitting on?" are different numbers). Case header, board rows, and the money section show **Customer ฿X · Insurer ฿Y**, collapsing to a single figure on single-payer cases so the common all-self-pay visit stays uncluttered; the blended total (their sum) drives board membership. Payment therefore records its payer side at entry (defaulted CUSTOMER — one extra field). The deliberate simplification: **no per-Job allocation** — a Payment lands in its payer's bucket on the case, nothing finer. Honest cost: a deductible (ค่าเสียหายส่วนแรก — the customer pays the first slice of an insurer Job) cannot be split within one Job; recorded as a customer Payment, the blended total stays correct but the customer side shows overpaid and the insurer side short by exactly the deductible — visible, explainable in the note, flagged for the interview checklist (if deductibles are routine, Job grows a customer-share field later, additively). Overpaid sides display as "overpaid ฿X", never a bare minus.
- **Payment mutability — approved: append-only with a one-way void; corrections re-enter.** Payment joins the append-only family (Quotation, JobAuthorization, CaseEvent, LineUpdate): rows are created complete and no update path exists for amount, method, payer, date, or note. The one mutable surface is the `LineUpdatePhoto.publicToken` precedent: **`voidedAt` + `voidedByStaffId` + `voidReason`, settable once, Manager-only** (the Cancel/price-override governance tier), never clearable. A mistyped ฿21,000 is corrected by void-with-reason + re-record; balances and spending sum only non-voided rows; voided rows stay on the case's payment list struck through with who and why. The internal timeline shows the whole story in order — `PAYMENT_RECORDED` (amount, method, payer), `PAYMENT_VOIDED` (amount, reason), `PAYMENT_RECORDED` again — so "did we actually receive this?" stays answerable months later. (Rejected: a reversing counter-entry, accounting's classic — keeps rows fully immutable but puts negative Payments into the schema and the UI, forces every consumer to pair reversals with originals, and the staff aren't accountants; the void flag buys the same auditability without any of that. Also rejected: plain editable rows — against the grain of every money-adjacent record in the schema.)
- **Follow-up representation — approved: stored rows with their own state, minted inside the Mark-delivered transaction.** The fact that makes stored rows safe: **delivery freezes the work record** (the M3/M4/M5 guards) — after handover no Job can be declined, reverted, or re-authorized and no Finding added — so the candidate set is final at exactly the moment that matters, and rows minted then can never drift from their sources. Before delivery nothing is minted, and rightly so: a declined Job can still be re-quoted and authorized mid-visit. Mint rule: one FollowUp per `DECLINED` Job (title + quoted price snapshotted), one per wear Finding (`DUE_SOON` / `NEEDS_WORK`) that never became authorized work — a Finding pointing at a declined Job is covered by that Job's row, never double-minted. The row is deliberately **mutable worklist state, not a history record** — status, snooze date, last action — while history lands where history lives: every transition writes its typed CaseEvent (M5's one-table ruling covers operational events, and chasing is operational). Cost at scale: none that matters — the worklist is a single indexed `[shopId, status]` table scan that sorts and paginates like any table. (Rejected: deriving the list on read — "mark contacted / snooze / drop" needs stored state regardless, which then bolts CRM columns onto Job and Finding; the list becomes a two-source UNION with per-item latest-state lookups that fights pagination and sorting; and M5's decision 1 already chose materialized simple reads over read-time composition.) A case never delivered never mints — acceptable, it is still visible on the board. Pre-M7 delivered cases on staging get the idempotent backfill script — unlike M5's no-backfill this fabricates no history, it materializes present state from frozen data.
- **How a follow-up reaches the customer — approved: no new message path; the delivered-case rule is re-scoped instead.** `LineUpdate.caseId` stays required — the seam M6 flagged stays closed, because a follow-up send *is* about its source case ("your windshield from the March visit — the quote still stands") and belongs on that case's Customer Timeline. What actually gives is the delivered-case lock, re-scoped rather than repealed: **delivery freezes the work record — Jobs, Findings, Quotations, statuses (exactly what decision 4 leans on) — but money and messages stay appendable**, because both genuinely happen after handover: insurers pay weeks late (M7 cannot exist without Payments landing on delivered cases — the carve-out is forced regardless), and most follow-up-worthy cases are delivered. Both are append-only records, so appending them rewrites nothing; the freeze's purpose — history cannot be rewritten — survives intact. Concretely: the composer un-disables on Delivered cases (M6's "Delivered cases keep their Customer Timeline read-only" is superseded by this ruling, and CONTEXT.md's "closed at delivery" gains the precise wording); opening a follow-up deep-links to the source case's composer with a follow-up-specific Thai draft; a successful send from that path auto-marks CONTACTED — bookkeeping caused by a human's send, the ADR-003-safe direction, and the manual "mark contacted" remains for phone contact. (Rejected: widening `caseId` to nullable for customer-level sends — a schema loosening, a second timeline surface on the customer page, and a weakened same-case pin, all to avoid re-scoping a guard that Payments force open anyway.)
- **Customer spending & the history split — approved: spending counts only the person's own Payments; insurer money never enters it.** CONTEXT.md is literal — "the sum of that person's real Payments" — and the insurer's transfer is neither the person's money nor their spending; blending would let one big claim make a one-visit customer look like a top client. Insurer receipts stay visible (case money section, visit context on the customer page) but never move the spending number; a per-insurer view is later reporting, out of M7. A CUSTOMER Payment is credited to the case's contact Customer at entry — MVP does not ask who physically handed over the cash (a relative paying at pickup mis-credits; interview checklist). The history split lands exactly as CONTEXT.md draws it: the **Customer page is relationship data** — visits, spending, payment rows, Follow-ups, LINE link — and keeps a sold car's visits and spending (the visit happened with this person); the **Vehicle page is physical truth** — every case chronologically across owners: work done, parts replaced, photos — and shows **no money anywhere**, so a car's new owner's view never reveals what the previous owner paid. Case pages stay full-detail for staff; the split governs the two history views, not access control.

## Done when
Fresh clone → migrate → seed → log in → run the M2–M6 flow to a case carrying four Jobs: a customer-paid repaint (authorized), an insurer-paid panel job (authorized, free-text insurer), a declined windshield replacement with a quoted price, and a brake-pads wear Finding left ungrouped. Record a ฿5,000 customer deposit (transfer) mid-repair → the header shows the split balance with the customer side reduced; the board row is unchanged (no Balance due for open cases). Complete the work → the case flips Ready → the **Ready for pickup** row shows the amount to collect at handover. Record the customer's remaining cash at pickup → customer side clears; **Mark delivered** → the case leaves the work groups and files under **Balance due** showing only the insurer side, in red, with days since delivered — and the delivery mints exactly two FollowUps (windshield with its quoted price; brake pads), which appear in `/followups`. Record the insurer's transfer on the delivered case (allowed — the re-scoped guard) → balance zero → the case leaves the board entirely. Mistype a payment (฿21,000 for ฿12,000) → Advisor void is refused server-side; Manager voids with a reason → the row shows struck-through with who/why, `PAYMENT_VOIDED` lands red on the internal timeline, the balance resurrects and the case returns to Balance due; re-record correctly → gone again. Editing any payment field is impossible anywhere. In `/followups`: open the windshield follow-up → the delivered case's composer opens pre-filled with a Thai draft naming the declined work and its quoted price → edit, send (fake transport) → the Update lands on that case's Customer Timeline, the follow-up flips CONTACTED by itself, and the linked CaseEvents appear. Mark the brake-pads one contacted by phone with a note; snooze it to next month → it leaves the actionable view; reopen and drop it with a reason → every transition on the timeline. The customer page shows spending equal to exactly their own non-voided payments — the insurer transfer visible on the visit but moving nothing — plus visits, payments, and both follow-ups with their states. Re-link the vehicle to a new primary Customer (M2 flow) → the vehicle page still shows the full repair record with photos across both owners and no money anywhere; the old owner's page keeps the visit and the spending. Adding a Job, Finding, or Quotation to the delivered case is still refused — the freeze holds for work. Everything usable in TH and EN; the follow-up draft stays Thai in both. Tenant-guard tests — extended to Payment, FollowUp, and every new link — pass. PR opened for founder review.
