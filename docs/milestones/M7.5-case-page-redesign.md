# M7.5 — Case page & board redesign

The legibility milestone. Goal: the Repair Case page stops being a wall of forms and chips a stranger cannot read, and becomes a page that answers two questions in seconds — **where is this car, and what's my next move?** M2–M7 built the machinery; M7.5 makes it speak. The founder's verdict on the current page (2026-08-27): too wordy, everything sticks together, badge soup in the header, job cards read as forms, and the workflow makes you figure out what to do by yourself. Every fix below was grilled and ruled in the 2026-08-27 design session; the rulings live as **Stage** in CONTEXT.md and **D-6 – D-10** in DESIGN.md — this brief sequences them into buildable scope. No schema changes, no permission changes, no new capture flows: presentation and one shared derivation helper.

**Branch:** `m7-5-case-page-redesign` · **Gate:** a newcomer reads a case's situation and next step in seconds; founder approves the rebuilt page on real cases at every Stage.

## Scope (in)
1. **Stage derivation — one helper, shared everywhere.** Extend lib/case-flow.ts with `stageFor(caseStatus, jobs, balance)`: the seven board groups plus the two Delivered flavors (**Balance due** while blended balance > 0, settled otherwise). `boardGroupFor()` becomes a view of the same derivation so board and case page can never disagree. Unit tests pin the attention-first precedence and both Delivered flavors.
2. **Next-action derivation.** A pure helper mapping Stage (+ case facts) to at most one primary and one secondary action, per D-6: In assessment cascades (no Findings → Open inspection; ungrouped Findings → Group into Jobs; unpriced Jobs → Set prices); Awaiting authorization → Record authorization, with Issue quotation suggested while no Quotation covers the Proposed Jobs; Waiting → no button, render the blocker ("2 parts due Aug 30" from Part Lines); In QC → Record QC result; Ready → Record payment (amount to collect) + Mark delivered; Balance due → Record payment. A suggestion, never a wizard — every existing action stays reachable where it lives today.
3. **Header rebuild (D-6, D-9, D-10).** One identity-plus-state card: car photo thumbnail (first walkaround photo, body-type icon fallback) · plate · make/model/color · contact name + phone (linked) — then the stage spine (`Assessment → Authorization → Work → Ready → Delivered`, current stage lit, sub-state written beneath) with the next-action strip and **one** money line. The lifecycle badge, per-status rollup chips, and due chips leave the header; the old Vehicle/Contact cards dissolve into it (province, odometer, primary-vs-contact demoted to small text or the linked pages). Mark ready / Mark delivered fold into the next-action strip where their preconditions already gate them.
4. **Job cards rebuilt as records (D-7).** Collapsed row: title · **one** status chip · payer and price as plain text (D-8 strict). Expanded: only what the status warrants — Proposed = the offer + authorize/decline; active = working view (technician, parts spread with ETAs, transitions, photos); Completed = receipt, no inputs; Cancelled/Declined = one line with the reason. Field edits behind a per-card **Edit** toggle; transitions, authorization recording, and part-arrival stay always-visible controls. Empty scaffolding never renders — small add affordances instead of headings over nothing. Parts table spread while active, one summary line once terminal.
5. **Skeleton & shrinking (D-10).** Fixed order Header → Inspection → Jobs → Money → Customer Updates → Activity. Inspection collapses to one line once Jobs exist; Money is one line until Ready/Balance due, then the M7 ledger; Activity opens as "last event" + expand; the walkaround grid folds into the Inspection area. No tabs, no reordering.
6. **Quiet-rulebook pass over both pages (D-8) + copy diet.** One chip per list row (chips = workflow states only); counts become sentences with at most a tinted word; the all-caps eyebrow idiom retires in favor of quiet label–value pairs where a label earns its place; type scale gains real steps; section seams gain air; provenance lines (Created …, recorded-by) demote to the expanded/на-demand layer. Every touched string re-worded short in **both** message files through the M1 i18n plumbing — this is the case-page/board slice of copy work; M8's whole-app bilingual pass stays M8's.
7. **Board identity pass (D-9) — grouping untouched.** Rows gain the car-photo thumbnail and the same one-chip discipline; a light density review so the page stops feeling empty (row height, secondary text) without adding new data or changing D-2 groups, precedence, or the Balance-due rendering.

## Scope (out — do not build)
- **Light theme or aesthetic reopen** — D-1/D-3 reaffirmed (D-8); the ClaimTrack reference contributes organization only.
- **Schema, permissions, or flow changes** — no new models, no transition edits, no auth changes. The freeze rules, QC gate, and Manager tiers render differently, never behave differently.
- **New capture flows** — the car photo is the existing first walkaround shot; no "beauty shot" step.
- **Inspection screen, check-in, customers, follow-ups, settings, quotation pages** — only the case page, its board, and the shared derivation are in scope. (The inspection *summary section* on the case page is in; the inspection screen itself is not.)
- **Claim records** (M8), **print styles** (M8), **whole-app bilingual pass** (M8), **reporting** (LATER).
- **Component library changes** — shadcn/lucide stay; no new UI dependencies.

## Decisions — ruled by the founder 2026-08-27
Grilled and documented in the design session; recorded where decisions live rather than restated here:
- **Stage** minted as canonical language; header speaks it exclusively — [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) + D-6. Rollup demoted to Jobs-section detail.
- **Quotation is never a gate to authorization** (walk-ins agree on the spot) — CONTEXT.md Job entry; shapes the Awaiting-authorization next-action.
- **Edit is explicit; actions are not edits** — D-7 (founder chose the Edit-button model over edit-on-focus and drawers).
- **One chip per row; labels whisper; dark stays** — D-8 (ruled against the light reference's skin, for its organization).
- **The car's real photo over brand logos** — D-9 (three same-model cars in the yard defeat a logo).
- **Fixed skeleton + shrinking over tabs/reordering** — D-10 (muscle memory over cleverness; Jobs stay above Money).
- **This milestone supersedes M8's former "case-page density pass" clause** — the Aug 27 founder call that spawned it ("the Job card reads as a form, not a summary") is fully absorbed by D-7/D-8; M8 keeps Claims, print, bilingual pass, and deploy.

## Done when
Seed (or stage locally) one case per Stage and walk them as a stranger: **In assessment** (fresh check-in) shows the spine at Assessment with "Open inspection"; after Findings exist the action reads "Group into Jobs"; after grouping, "Set prices" until every Job is priced. **Awaiting authorization** shows "Issue quotation" as the suggested path while none covers the Proposed Jobs, and "Record authorization" always works without one (the walk-in). **Waiting — parts** shows no button but the blocking part and its ETA in the header. **Ready** leads with the amount to collect and offers Record payment + Mark delivered. **Delivered — balance due** lights the full spine, states the owed side, offers Record payment; **delivered-settled** reads as a closed record roughly one screen tall. On every one of them: the header contains the car's photo, plate, contact, exactly one money line, and **zero** rollup/due chips; each job row carries exactly one chip; a Cancelled job renders as one quiet line; no empty PARTS/PHOTOS scaffolding exists anywhere; every remaining editable field sits behind a per-card Edit toggle while Start work / Send to QC / QC result / Record authorization / part-arrival remain one tap; Inspection, Money, and Activity sections shrink and expand per D-10. The board shows car thumbnails, one chip per row, unchanged grouping. Both languages read naturally with the shortened copy; money renders tabular through `formatBaht`. `stageFor` unit tests pass, including both Delivered flavors and attention-first precedence. The founder opens the six staged cases and approves the look of each — the gate is their eyes, not a checklist.
