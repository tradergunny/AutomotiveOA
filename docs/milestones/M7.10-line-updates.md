# M7.10 — LINE Updates: automatic sending

The trust milestone. Goal: the customer's LINE becomes a live, credible view of their car's visit without anyone at the desk remembering to write — check-in, quotation, work started, waiting for parts, each finished job with its photos, final check, ready, thank you — while the shop's internal narrative (QC bounces, reverts, cancellations, price changes) never leaks. Decided in the 2026-09-10 grill session: [ADR-007](../adr/ADR-007-system-sent-customer-updates.md) supersedes ADR-003; CONTEXT.md gains **Milestone message** and **Progress notice**; DESIGN.md gains **D-29**.

**Branch:** `m7-10-line-updates` · **Gate:** on the staged shop, one case walked check-in → quotation → work → QC → Ready → Delivered leaves the whole customer-side story in the fake transport's outbox with no compose step; a customer linked late receives one catch-up; the case page's Customer Updates section is a compact log, not a chat.

**How to work this brief.** It is cut into eight steps. Each step is one commit (or a few), ends with `npm test`, `npm run lint` and `npx tsc --noEmit` green, and has its own tests written *before* the wiring it tests. Do not start step N+1 with step N red. Steps 1–4 are pure and DB-level and can be verified entirely by tests; steps 5–7 are wiring and UI and are verified by tests plus the fake outbox; step 8 is the walk. If a step turns out to need a decision the brief does not make, stop and ask rather than guessing — the never-list in ADR-007 is closed.

## Scope (in) — the eight steps

### Step 1 — Schema: kinds and not-sent rows
`LineUpdateKind` enum: `CHECKIN · QUOTATION · WORK_STARTED · WAITING_PARTS · PARTS_ARRIVED · JOB_COMPLETED · IN_QC · READY · DELIVERED · CATCH_UP · FREEFORM`. `LineUpdate.kind` required; migration backfills existing rows to `QUOTATION` where `quotationId` is set, else `FREEFORM`. `LineDeliveryStatus` gains `NOT_SENT`; `LineUpdate.lineUserId` becomes nullable (a `noIdentity` block has no userId to snapshot) and `errorCode` carries the gate reason on a `NOT_SENT` row (`noIdentity` / `unfollowed` / `notConnected` / `cryptoUnavailable`). `sentByStaffId` stays required and is documented as *the Staff whose act caused the Update* — a Progress notice is caused by the QC pass, a catch-up by the link. `LineUpdate.jobId` nullable (same-shop composite FK) so a `JOB_COMPLETED` row names its Job for the coalescing guard and the log. `scripts/stage-cases.ts` writes kinds. Tenant guard classification unchanged in kind, extended in tests.
**Tests:** migration applies on a fresh database; tenant-guard suite passes with the new columns; a `NOT_SENT` row can exist with null `lineUserId`; `cases:stage` runs and its rows carry kinds.

### Step 2 — Wording: one builder per kind
`lib/line-draft.ts` gains a pure builder per system kind, all Thai, all taking `{ shopName, customerName, plate, reference }` plus their own facts (`JOB_COMPLETED`: the Job title; `WAITING_PARTS`: the earliest Part Line ETA or "soon" when none; `READY`: the Customer-owed satang, omitted when zero; `CATCH_UP`: the current Jobs, reusing `buildDraftBody`'s status lines) and an optional `note` appended as a trailing paragraph, trimmed, capped at 300 characters. `buildDraftBody` survives as the `FREEFORM` seed and the catch-up body. `buildQuotationBody` untouched.
**Tests (extend `tests/line-transport.test.ts` or a new `tests/line-wording.test.ts`):** every builder's output contains no internal vocabulary (`/QC|WAITING|IN_PROGRESS|COMPLETED|DECLINED|CANCELLED/`); `READY` names the customer amount and never the insurer's; `DELIVERED` contains no baht; `WAITING_PARTS` renders the date in Thai and copes with no ETA; the note appears last and is capped; every builder ends with the shop name.

### Step 3 — The system send seam
`lib/line-updates.ts`: `sendSystemUpdate(db, { actor, caseId, kind, note?, jobId?, photoIds? })`. Loads the case and contact, runs `lineGateFor`; on a block **records a `NOT_SENT` row** (no push, no photo tokens, `CaseEvent` `LINE_UPDATE_NOT_SENT` — a new `CaseEventType`); otherwise builds the messages (text + up to four images, newest first, JPEG/PNG only, via the existing `/api/line/photo/[token]` route) and calls `deliverLineUpdate` with the kind. **Never throws**: every failure is caught, logged, and returned as a result. This is the only function steps 5 and 6 call.
**Tests (DB-backed, fake transport):** blocked customer → one `NOT_SENT` row with the reason, nothing in the outbox; linked customer → one `SENT` row, exactly one outbox line with the expected text and image count; a transport error → `FAILED` row, tokens null; photos not JPEG/PNG are skipped; a thrown error inside is swallowed and reported, never propagated.

### Step 4 — Trigger derivation, pure
`lib/line-triggers.ts`: `noticesFor(input): LineUpdateKind[]` — given the case before and after an act (`caseStatus`, Jobs with `status` + `waitingReason`, the acted-on Job and action) and the case's send history (`kinds already sent`, `last JOB_COMPLETED sentAt`), returns the kinds to send, in order. Encodes every rule from CONTEXT.md: `WORK_STARTED` once, on the first Job reaching `IN_PROGRESS`; `WAITING_PARTS` when Stage enters Waiting with reason Parts (not Paint booth / Technician / Other); `PARTS_ARRIVED` when a Parts wait ends by resuming; `IN_QC` on Stage entry to In QC; `JOB_COMPLETED` on a `QC_PASS` — **unless** that pass makes the case Ready (then only `READY`), and **unless** a `JOB_COMPLETED` was sent within the last hour (coalesce: skip, and the later message names nothing extra — the Ready or the next completion carries the state); `READY` on case status entering `READY` from any act; nothing for `QC_FAIL`, `CANCEL`, `SET_WAITING` with a non-Parts reason, reason changes, reverts.
**Tests (`tests/line-triggers.test.ts`, the heart of the milestone — be exhaustive):** one test per rule above, one per never-list item asserting an empty result, the Ready-subsumes case, the coalescing window at 59 and 61 minutes, the two-Job case where the second `IN_PROGRESS` sends nothing, a Parts wait that changes to Technician sending nothing, a revoked-then-re-reached Ready sending `READY` again (a second Ready message is correct: the customer was told, then work reopened, then it is ready again).

### Step 5 — Wire the Progress notices and Ready
`transitionJob`, `revertJobStep`, `markCaseReady`: after the transaction commits, compute `noticesFor` from the before/after snapshots and call `sendSystemUpdate` once per kind, in order, then `revalidateCase`. `JOB_COMPLETED` attaches the Job's own photos. `markCaseReady` sends `READY`. `revertJobStep` sends nothing but must still run clean. The optional note: `markCaseReady`'s confirm dialog gains a "note to the customer" field (D-23's small-dialog shape); QC pass does not (no human is addressing the customer there).
**Tests (DB-backed, fake transport, extend `tests/jobs-flow.test.ts` or a new `tests/line-flow.test.ts`):** walk one case with two Jobs through start → waiting(Parts) → resume → QC → pass, pass and assert the outbox sequence is exactly `WORK_STARTED, WAITING_PARTS, PARTS_ARRIVED, IN_QC, READY` (the last pass subsumes its completion); a QC fail adds nothing; a transport failure leaves the Job transitioned and a `FAILED` row; a customer with no contact leaves the Job transitioned and `NOT_SENT` rows.

### Step 6 — Wire the Milestones: Check-in, Delivered, catch-up
`performCheckin` sends `CHECKIN` after its transaction, with the wizard's new optional note; a check-in that consumed a LINE Arrival is reachable immediately, one from nothing is usually `NOT_SENT`. `markCaseDelivered` sends `DELIVERED` with its dialog's optional note. `linkLineContactToCustomer`'s callers (Settings inbox, Customer page, and the Arrival path inside check-in) send one `CATCH_UP` per open case in `touchedCaseIds` — **except** when the link happened inside `performCheckin`, where the `CHECKIN` message is the first contact and a catch-up would be a duplicate. `sendQuotation` gains only the kind.
**Tests:** check-in from a LINE Arrival → `CHECKIN` sent; check-in from nothing → `CHECKIN` not-sent; linking that customer later → one `CATCH_UP` per open case, none for delivered cases, none when linked during check-in; Delivered → `DELIVERED` with the note; the unlink path sends nothing.

### Step 7 — The case page: log and dialog (D-29)
`customer-timeline.tsx` becomes the compact log: one line per Update (kind label from i18n, time, sent / not sent with reason / failed, photo count, the note's presence), expanding to the full Thai body and thumbnails; opens as last-message-plus-expand like Activity. **Send a message** button opens `send-message-dialog.tsx` in the Send quotation shape: recipient in the title, textarea seeded by `buildDraftBody`, photo picks, one Send, the gate's reason inline when blocked. Preview and arm-then-confirm removed. `sendLineUpdate` writes kind `FREEFORM`. The Follow-up deep link (`?followup=`) opens the dialog pre-filled with the chase draft. All new strings in both message files; the kind labels are staff i18n, the bodies stay Thai data. Built in D-27's language with local utilities, like the board.
**Tests:** `sendLineUpdate` still passes its validation tests and now writes `FREEFORM`; the page renders a not-sent entry with its reason; the dialog opens pre-filled from the follow-up link. Visual check by the founder against the brief's one sentence: it must not take the space it took before.

### Step 8 — Docs and the walk
`scripts/stage-cases.ts` stages one case with the full sequence in its outbox; README's "LINE in dev" section shows how to read the outbox as a story; LINE-SETUP.md Part 5 is rewritten around what the customer now receives; MILESTONES.md's M7.10 row gets its PR link. Then the **Done when** walk below, recorded in MILESTONES.md.

## Scope (out — do not build)
- **Any kind not in the enum above.** Adding one is a CONTEXT.md change, argued in the open (ADR-007).
- **The silence guard** ([LATER.md](../LATER.md)) — no cron, no scheduler, no unattended sends.
- **Retrying failed pushes** — a `FAILED` row is visible; the dialog is the retry.
- **Per-Shop editable templates, Flex messages, rich menus** ([LATER.md](../LATER.md)).
- **Replaying missed messages on link** — one catch-up, describing now.
- **Sending walkaround or Finding photos automatically** — evidence, never a greeting (CONTEXT.md **Photo**).
- **Restyling anything outside the Customer Updates section and the two dialogs** — M7.11.
- **Reading replies** — ADR-005 stands; the webhook takes identity and nothing else.

## Decisions — ruled by the founder 2026-09-10
Recorded where decisions live: [ADR-007](../adr/ADR-007-system-sent-customer-updates.md), CONTEXT.md **Milestone message** / **Progress notice** / **LINE Update** / **Photo**, DESIGN.md **D-29**, LATER.md (silence guard). In one line each:
- Milestone messages are act-carried; Progress notices are event-triggered; the never-list is closed.
- "Level 2" now (evidence-driven, each finished Job with photos), "Level 3" (silence guard) later.
- A blocked send is recorded as not-sent; linking sends one catch-up.
- Wording is the system's; one optional note per Milestone act; templates stay deferred.
- The composer becomes a dialog; the Customer Timeline becomes a compact log; preview and double press retire; it must be small.
- M7.10 ships before the restyle.
