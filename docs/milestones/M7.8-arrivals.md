# M7.8 — Arrivals: customer self-service check-in

The front-desk milestone, revisited. Goal: the customer's own knowledge — who they are, which car, what is wrong — stops being re-keyed by the advisor and stops needing a hand-match in the LINE contacts inbox. A customer at the counter submits an **Arrival** from their phone; the advisor pulls it into the check-in wizard, which stays the only place a Customer, Vehicle, or Repair Case is ever created; and when the Arrival came through LINE, confirming it links the LINE identity in the same transaction, so the resulting case can be sent its first LINE Update with no manual link. The advisor's wizard keeps every capability it has today, because a tow that arrives without its owner is checked in from nothing. Decided in the 2026-09-08 grill session and recorded as [ADR-006](../adr/ADR-006-customer-self-service-arrival.md) and CONTEXT.md **Arrival**; this brief sequences those rulings into buildable scope.

**Branch:** `m7-8-arrivals` · **Gate:** a customer at the counter submits an Arrival from their phone, the advisor consumes it in the wizard, and the resulting case can be sent a LINE Update with no manual link.

## Scope (in)
1. **Schema — `Arrival`, `ArrivalStatus`, two columns on `Shop`, two on `ShopLineChannel`.**
   - **Arrival** — `shopId`; `status` (`WAITING / CHECKED_IN / DISMISSED`, default `WAITING`); what the customer typed, normalized the way check-in normalizes it: `name`, `phone` (digits, via `lib/normalize.ts`), `plate` (normalized), `bodyType` (nullable), `note` (what's wrong), `odometerKm` (nullable), `locale` (`th`/`en`, the form's language at submit); the recognized path's choice: nullable `vehicleId` (same-shop composite FK) when the customer tapped one of their own cars; identity: nullable `lineUserId`, `lineDisplayName`, `linePictureUrl` — **written only from a server-verified ID token** (decision 1), never from the request body; `submittedAt`; resolution: nullable `caseId` (same-shop composite FK, set when consumed), `handledByStaffId` (same-shop composite FK), `handledAt`. Rows are never edited by the customer after submit and never deleted by the app; stale is derived on read (§5), not stored — the Follow-up snooze idiom.
   - **Shop** gains `arrivalToken String? @unique` (the public form key, 128-bit random, base64url) and `arrivalTokenRotatedAt`. Null means the Shop has not enabled the form; the seed leaves it null.
   - **ShopLineChannel** gains `liffId String?` and `loginChannelId String?`. Neither is a secret (both appear in the customer's own LIFF URL and token), so they are stored plain — ADR-004's encryption is for the two values that can send as the Shop, and these cannot.
   - Classified in `lib/tenant.ts` (Arrival→{Vehicle, RepairCase, Staff} same-shop composite FKs); tenant-guard tests extended. One migration.
2. **The public Arrival page** (`/a/[token]`, unauthenticated, `noindex`, 404 on an unknown or rotated token — the `/q` terms). Always-light, Thai-first with an English toggle, phone-sized. Resolves the Shop through one unscoped read (`resolveArrivalShop(token)` in `lib/line-public.ts`), then `forShop()`. Shows the Shop's name and nothing else about it. **Write-only:** the page never answers whether a phone or plate is known, and a submission's confirmation screen echoes only what the customer typed plus "please see the front desk". First-time fields, exactly these: name, phone, plate (required); body type as two icons, sedan or pickup (required); what's wrong (required); odometer (optional). No company, province, make, model, colour. Submission is a server action on the page (the proxy matcher gains `a/`), which validates with the check-in normalizers, enforces the cap (§5), and creates the `Arrival` — no Customer, Vehicle, or case, ever. A per-source rate limit (the webhook's map idiom, keyed by token plus client address, a few per minute) sits in front of it.
3. **The same page inside LINE.** When the Shop's channel carries a `liffId`, the page loads the LIFF SDK and initializes; outside LINE (or when LIFF fails to initialize) it behaves exactly as §2. Inside LINE, the page obtains the **ID token** and sends it with every server call; the server verifies it against LINE (`oauth2/v2.1/verify` with the Shop's `loginChannelId`) and takes `sub` as the userId, plus name and picture from the token's profile claims. The client never sends a userId, and the server never trusts one (decision 1). **Recognition:** `recognizeArrival(token, idToken)` returns the linked Customer's **vehicles only** — plate, body type, make/model/colour as display text, and their ids — when a `LineContact` with that userId is linked to a Customer; an unlinked or unknown userId gets `null` and the write-only form. Nothing else about the Customer crosses: no name prefill beyond what the ID token itself carries, no history, no cases, no balances. The recognized form is: tap a car or "another car" (which reveals the plate and body-type fields), what's wrong, odometer. Submitting either way stores the verified identity on the Arrival. The verifier joins `lib/line.ts` as `verifyIdToken` with a live driver and a fake driver (decision 4).
4. **The queue on the check-in page.** Above section 01, a strip that exists only when the Shop has waiting Arrivals: one row each — name, plate, the first line of what's wrong, how long ago, a LINE mark when identity came along — newest first, with a dismiss control per row (`dismissArrival`, records the Staff and time). The Check-in nav item carries the waiting count. Tapping a row **pulls the Arrival into the wizard**: the phone and plate fields fill, run their lookups, and sections 01–03 populate from the result. No Case Board change: an Arrival is not a case.
5. **Cap and staleness.** Waiting is `status = WAITING` and `submittedAt` within the last 24 hours; older rows are stale, excluded from the queue and the count, and left in place. The form refuses new submissions when the Shop has 50 waiting (a friendly "please see the front desk"), so a flood is bounded.
6. **Consumption — the gateway.** `performCheckin` accepts an optional `arrivalId`. The Arrival's **visit fields are authoritative**: note and odometer land in the wizard as typed and submit as edited. Its **identity fields are advisory**: when the phone finds a Customer, that record is the contact and the typed name shows beside it as "customer wrote: …" with a one-tap **Update name**; when the plate finds a Vehicle, its details win and the typed body type shows the same way; the existing keep-or-relink ownership control is unchanged. When neither is found, the typed values prefill the new-customer and new-vehicle fields exactly as if the advisor had typed them. **The one hard stop:** the Arrival carries a verified userId whose `LineContact` is already linked to a Customer other than the one the phone found (or the phone found no one and the linked Customer's phone differs). The wizard shows both people and refuses to submit until the advisor picks the contact; `performCheckin` re-checks and refuses with `lineIdentityConflict` if the choice is missing. On submit, in the **same transaction** as today: the Arrival flips to `CHECKED_IN` with `caseId`, `handledBy`, `handledAt`; a verified userId upserts its `LineContact` (display name and picture refreshed from the token) and links it to the contact Customer through the same code path as `linkLineContact` — replacing any previous link, and writing `LINE_CUSTOMER_LINKED` on the new case and the Customer's other open cases, as that action does today. An Arrival already `CHECKED_IN` or `DISMISSED` refuses (`arrivalGone`); a stale one may still be consumed if the advisor opens it from a direct link, since staleness is only about the queue. No new `CaseEventType`: the case's provenance is the Arrival row, and the case page's header line reads "checked in from the customer's Arrival" when `Arrival.caseId` points at it.
7. **Auto-lookup.** Phone and plate look themselves up when the advisor pauses typing (a short debounce, a valid phone or a non-empty plate) and on blur; Enter still works. The two Look up buttons retire. Everything else about the wizard's behaviour is unchanged; this is the mechanical fix from the session, bundled here because it is the same complaint.
8. **Settings.** A new **Arrivals** panel, Manager-only to change and read-only for Advisors, following the LINE panel's shape: **Enable the form** mints `arrivalToken`; the panel shows the plain URL with an in-app QR to print, **Rotate link** (mints a new token, records `arrivalTokenRotatedAt`, the old URL dies immediately), and **Disable** (nulls the token; Arrivals already recorded stay). Under the connected LINE channel: two plain fields, LIFF ID and Login channel ID, with the LIFF endpoint URL to paste into the LINE console shown beside them, and a short bilingual checklist mirroring LINE-SETUP Part 6. With no `liffId` the LINE door is simply closed; the plain door works alone.
9. **Onboarding docs.** [LINE-SETUP.md](../LINE-SETUP.md) gains **Part 6 — Let customers register from LINE** (create a LINE Login channel **under the same provider as the OA** — the reason stated plainly: LINE gives the same person the same id only within one provider — add a LIFF app whose endpoint is the URL from Settings, scopes `profile` and `openid`, copy the LIFF ID and Channel ID into Settings) and **Part 7 — The counter** (the OA's add-friend QR on the poster, a rich menu button named ลงทะเบียน pointing at the LIFF URL, configured by hand in OA Manager; the plain QR as the fallback for customers who refuse LINE). README's "LINE in dev" section gains the fake identity (decision 4). `.env.example` needs nothing new.
10. **i18n & copy.** Every staff-facing string bilingual through the M1 plumbing: the queue strip, "customer wrote", the conflict stop, the Settings panel, every error. The customer form's copy is **Thai-first with an English toggle** — customer-facing like the quotation document, but a form the customer must understand, so both languages ship; the toggle is on the page, not derived from the staff locale. The form's copy lives in the message files under its own namespace, not in `lib/line-draft.ts`, because it is UI, not a message.
11. **Tests.** A DB suite for: token resolve and rotation (old token 404s), the cap and 24-hour staleness, consumption atomicity (a conflict or a bad Arrival writes nothing — no Customer, no case, Arrival still `WAITING`), consumption linking the LineContact and replacing a previous link, and the conflict refusal. A unit suite for `verifyIdToken`'s fake driver and for the rule that a body-supplied userId is ignored. `recognizeArrival` returns vehicles only, and `null` for an unlinked identity. Tenant-guard coverage of the new model, columns, and FKs — including that two Shops' Arrivals never cross and that a token resolves to exactly one Shop.
12. **Dev and staging tooling.** `npm run cases:stage` gains two waiting Arrivals on the pilot shop — one plain, one with a fake-verified identity that conflicts with a seeded link — so the walkthrough covers prefill, "customer wrote", and the hard stop. The fake `verifyIdToken` driver accepts tokens of the form `dev:U…` and returns that userId with a stand-in name, mirroring the `dev-` access-token convention; in development the form shows a small "LINE identity (dev)" field so the LIFF path can be walked without a LINE Login channel.

## Scope (out — do not build)
- **Any LINE message** at submission or confirmation — ADR-003 stands; the acknowledgement is the page itself and the advisor at the counter.
- **Tap-to-approve in LINE**, **rich menus and Flex messages** built by the app, **a customer portal** (history, balances, open cases on the customer's phone) — [LATER.md](../LATER.md). Recognition shows vehicles only; that boundary is in ADR-006.
- **Appointments or booking** ahead of arrival — an Arrival is for a car that is here.
- **Board changes**, **Customer/Vehicle edit flows** beyond the one-tap name update, **dedupe tooling** — a wrong-number Arrival is dismissed, not merged.
- **Automatic consumption** of any kind — no "auto-check-in when the plate is known", no auto-linking without the advisor's confirm.
- **Replacing ADR-005's manual link** — the contacts inbox stays for customers who never submit an Arrival.

## Decisions — ruled by the founder 2026-09-08
Recorded where decisions live rather than restated here:
- **Happy path (b), fallback (c); per visit; named Arrival** — CONTEXT.md **Arrival**, ADR-006.
- **Both doors, LINE identity included**, at the cost of one more onboarding part — ADR-006 and its consequences.
- **Write-only plain path; vehicles-only recognition on the linked LINE path** — ADR-006.
- **Identity advisory, visit authoritative, only the linked-elsewhere case hard-stops** — ADR-006.
- **Silent on LINE** — ADR-006, ADR-003 unchanged.
- **Rotatable token, a cap, 24-hour staleness** — ADR-006.
- **The field set, body type included; the queue lives on the check-in page, the board untouched** — this brief §2, §4.

### Calls made in this brief (veto at review)
Details the session did not reach, decided here so the build does not stall; each is reversible in a line:
1. **The userId on an Arrival comes only from a server-verified LINE ID token.** A client-supplied userId would let anyone at the counter get their LINE linked to someone else's Customer with one advisor confirm, and the next LINE Update would go to them. Verification needs the Login channel ID, which is why Settings stores it.
2. **LIFF ID and Login channel ID are stored plain.** Neither can send a message or verify a webhook; both are visible to every customer who opens the form. ADR-004 covers secrets, and these are not.
3. **Stale Arrivals are derived, not swept.** The queue filters on `submittedAt`; no job, no cron, no status flip. A stale row opened from a direct link can still be consumed, because the 24 hours is about the queue, not about validity.
4. **The ID-token verifier is a second driver behind `lib/line.ts`'s existing seam**, with a `dev:U…` fake, so the whole LINE door is walkable with no LINE Login channel, no tunnel, and no fees — the M6 rule.
5. **No new `CaseEventType`.** Check-in already stamps `checkedInAt` and `openedBy`; the Arrival row, pointing at the case, is the provenance. The LINE link made during consumption uses the existing `LINE_CUSTOMER_LINKED` event.
6. **The customer form is always-light**, like the `/q` document: inside LINE's in-app browser it is the whole page, and the dark app chrome never appears to a customer.
7. **The cap is 50 and the window is 24 hours** — sized for "no real shop ever queues fifty cars at the counter", easy to move later.

## Candidates raised, not ruled
Named during the session for later rulings; each one line, with what it buys:
- **"Tell the customer" on confirm** — open the LINE composer prefilled the moment a case opens with a linked contact, so the first Update is one tap (ADR-003 intact, a human still sends).
- **Dashboard nudge for waiting Arrivals** — a count on the board's header, not rows on the board, if advisors turn out not to sit on the check-in page.
- **Province on the form for Bangkok plates** — identical plates across provinces are real; today the advisor adds province by hand.
- **A photo from the customer** — one shot of the damage from their own phone, attached to the Arrival and carried into the case's walkaround. Tempting, but D-9 makes the first walkaround photo the car's face on the board, and that should stay the advisor's shot.
