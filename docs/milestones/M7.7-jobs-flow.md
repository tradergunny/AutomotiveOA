# M7.7 — Jobs flow: Offer → Work → Done

The journey milestone. Goal: the Jobs section of the Repair Case page stops being a chronological list of forms the advisor has to steer by themselves and becomes a flow that reads in time order — **Offer → Work → Done** — where every screen says what happens next, and the sittings between "car arrives" and "customer says yes" drop from six plus one authorization per Job to **four**: at the car, price, send, record the response. The founder's verdict on the M7.5 section (2026-09-02): uninstructed, out of place, a weird user journey. The redesign was drawn end to end on the [Jobs Flow Redesign canvas](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/d7794c26-d655-4dbe-ae41-4d9e30237562) (working artboards under [design/jobs-flow/](../design/jobs-flow/)) and ruled as **D-19 – D-25** in DESIGN.md, with **Offer** and **Response** minted in CONTEXT.md. This brief sequences those rulings into buildable scope. Unlike M7.5 it is **not** presentation-only: two server actions are new, one freeze point moves, and the schema grows two columns and one event type.

**Branch:** `m7-7-jobs-flow` · **Gate:** on a staged case a stranger prices the Offer, sends it, records the customer's answer and starts work without being told where anything is; the founder walks Offer → Work → Done on the staged cases and approves.

## Scope (in)
1. **Phase-organized section (D-19).** The Jobs section renders three phases in one fixed order — **Offer** (Proposed), **Work** (Authorized, Waiting, In progress, QC), **Done** (Completed, Declined, Cancelled). A phase with no members renders nothing; phase headings appear only when two or more phases have members. The section header carries "Jobs" and the count only; the per-status rollup becomes each phase's sub-line ("2 jobs · 1 waiting — parts · 1 in QC"), `JobRollupLine` surviving for board rows. A Delivered case shows Done rows alone, read-only.
2. **Accept fills the Offer (D-24).** `setFindingConfirmed(true)` on a Finding proposing work creates its Proposed Job in the same transaction — title derived from the zone or checklist label and the action words in the staff member's locale (shop data from then on, like a typed title), payer `CUSTOMER`, unpriced, `JOB_CREATED` event. A due-soon Finding creates nothing (`isGroupable`). Reopening (`setFindingConfirmed(false)`, `updateFinding`, photo edits, `removeFinding`) is allowed while the Job is Proposed, unpriced and covered by no Quotation line; the derived title follows while it still equals the last derivation. Once the line is priced or sent, every Finding mutation refuses with a `findingFrozen` error, as D-18's grouped check does today. `deleteJob` (Proposed only) now **reopens** its Findings — clears `confirmedAt` — instead of leaving them accepted and ungrouped. New action `mergeJobs(caseId, jobIds)`: all Proposed, same case, same payer; the oldest survives, Findings, part lines and photos move to it, the others are deleted; price kept only if exactly one part had one; recorded as `JOB_MERGED` events on the absorbed lines naming the survivor.
3. **The Offer table (D-19, D-21).** Columns: Job (title, with "2 findings" or "catalog" as a faint sub-word), Payer, Price. Grouped into two sub-tables when payers mix, the insurer's name titling the second. The price cell is live — quoted rows for everyone, catalog rows for the Manager — saving through `updateJobPrice` on blur. An expanded row shows fulfils chips (linking to the inspection), the note, a parts summary with Add part, photos with Add photo, **Edit** (title, payer, note, technician) and **Delete** (arm-then-confirm). Merge: a checkbox column appears once the Offer holds two or more lines; a Merge button joins the foot when two or more are ticked. Foot, left to right: **Add job** · the last-send line ("Q-1031 · sent Sep 2 via LINE" / "Not sent", plus "prices changed since" when stale) · **Send quotation** · **Record response**. Exactly one of the two right-hand buttons is primary, mirroring the header's move; the other is outline — Record response always stays reachable for the walk-in.
4. **Add job dialog (D-22).** Sources: **Standard service** (catalog list, price locked; Manager override afterwards as today) and **Custom** (title, optional price). Payer chips with insurer name. Buttons: Add job · Add and another · Cancel. Opened from the Offer foot and from the header strip. The `?group-finding=` deep link and the `GROUP_FINDINGS` action retire; the inspection screen keeps its Job chip.
5. **Record response (D-20).** One dialog per payer part: channel as a segmented control, Quotation shown (default the latest covering the part; "none" for the walk-in), a Yes/No pair per line (an unpriced line offers No only and says "needs a price"), an **All yes** shortcut, a note, the running "2 authorized · 1 declined", **Save N decisions**. New action `recordOfferResponse(caseId, {payerType, insurerName?, channel, quotationId?, note?, decisions})`: one transaction writing one `JobAuthorization` and one `JOB_AUTHORIZATION_RECORDED` event per decision, `applyCaseReadiness` once; refuses on any Job not Proposed, not this case's, not this payer's, or authorized without a price. The per-card authorization strip goes; Manager revert stays (⋯ on a Work card, ⋯ on a Declined row).
6. **Send quotation (D-25).** The dialog shows the payer part (a selector when mixed), its priced lines and total, a warning line for unpriced lines left behind, and **Send** (LINE) beside **Print**. New action `sendQuotation(caseId, {payerType, insurerName?})`: reuse the latest Quotation whose lines equal the part's priced lines at current prices, else snapshot a new version through the existing issue path (`QUOTATION_ISSUED`); mint `Quotation.publicToken` on first send; build the Thai body in `lib/line-draft.ts` (`buildQuotationBody`: greeting, number, one line per Job with price, total, link, shop name); push through the `sendLineUpdate` path, recording the `LineUpdate` with its new `quotationId` and the `LINE_UPDATE_SENT` / `LINE_UPDATE_FAILED` event. The Customer Timeline renders the update with the quotation label. Public route `app/q/[token]/page.tsx`: the always-light document for that version, unauthenticated, `noindex`, 404 on an unknown token; the staff document page links to it. Insurer parts: Print only. Send is blocked, with the composer's own explanations, when no channel is connected or no LINE identity is linked. Schema: `Quotation.publicToken String? @unique`, `LineUpdate.quotationId` (same-shop composite FK, nullable), `CaseEventType.JOB_MERGED` — one migration, classified in `lib/tenant.ts`.
7. **Work cards (D-23).** Collapsed row: chevron · title · one chip · technician · price. Active cards start expanded. Expanded: technician select (live, `updateJob`) · price and payer as text · **⋯** (Edit details = note; Add part; Add photo; Cancel job…; Revert step; Revert to proposed — each shown per permission, Manager-only ones faint for others) · the waiting blocker line when Waiting · the M7.5 parts table, unchanged · photos · one action row — Authorized: **Start work** / Waiting…; Waiting: **Resume work** / Change reason…; In progress: **Send to QC** / Waiting…; QC: **QC pass** (Manager, never the assignee; a hint otherwise) / QC fail… · one provenance line. Dialogs: Set waiting (reason segmented, Confirm), QC fail (required note), Cancel job (required note). `transitionJob` and `revertJobStep` are unchanged.
8. **Done rows (D-23).** Completed: title · chip · technician · price, expanding to the receipt (fulfils, parts cost, photos, QC by/when, authorization). Declined: date · channel · note · price faint, ⋯ Revert to proposed (Manager). Cancelled: date · by · note, ⋯ Revert step (Manager).
9. **Header next action (D-6 as amended).** `StageAction` becomes `OPEN_INSPECTION · SET_PRICES · SEND_QUOTATION · RECORD_RESPONSE · RECORD_QC · RECORD_PAYMENT · MARK_DELIVERED`. Cascade: In assessment — no Findings or unaccepted Findings → Open inspection, else nothing; Awaiting authorization — unpriced lines → Set prices, else unsent or stale → **Send quotation** primary with Record response secondary, else **Record response** primary. Set prices scrolls to the Offer and focuses the first empty cell; the other three open their dialogs, so the strip becomes a client component. The money line is unchanged.
10. **Copy, both languages.** Every new string through the M1 plumbing in both message files; the quotation message is Thai-first data in `lib/line-draft.ts`, not i18n copy. Renamed labels: Record response, Send quotation, Add job.
11. **Tests.** `case-flow.test.ts` for the new cascade; a DB suite for accept-creates-line, the freeze point, delete-reopens, and the merge price rule; `recordOfferResponse` atomicity (one bad decision writes nothing); `sendQuotation` version reuse versus new version; the public route's 200/404; tenant-guard coverage of the new column and FK.
12. **Backfill and staging.** `scripts/offer-backfill.ts` creates lines for accepted, ungrouped, work-proposing Findings on open cases (idempotent; dev and staging — the pilot is not live); `scripts/stage-cases.ts` gains a sent-Offer case and a response-recorded case so the walkthrough covers the new states.

## Decisions — ruled by the founder 2026-09-02
Recorded where decisions live rather than restated here:
- **The section is organized by phase**, and the payer answers the Offer as a set — D-19, D-20; **Offer** and **Response** in CONTEXT.md.
- **The price cell is the one live field outside Edit** — D-21.
- **One Add job dialog; header actions open dialogs** — D-22.
- **One primary next step per card; technician live; corrections in ⋯; interruptions as small dialogs** — D-23.
- **Accept fills the Offer; merge; the freeze point moves to priced-or-sent** — D-24, amending D-11 and D-18; CONTEXT.md Finding and Job.
- **Send quotation stamps the version and reaches the customer with a document link** — D-25, superseding M4's explicit Issue action; CONTEXT.md Quotation.

### Calls made in this brief (veto at review)
Details the canvas and rulings left open, decided here so the build does not stall; each is reversible in a line:
- **Merge keeps a price only if exactly one part had one.** Two priced panels are not their sum once they are one repaint.
- **Send covers the payer's priced lines and warns about the rest** rather than refusing — a placeholder line must not block a real offer.
- **The title stops following the Finding the moment it is retyped**, judged by comparing it with the last derivation — no extra column.
- **Insurer parts are Print only** — no push to an insurer ever, per CONTEXT.md.
- **`JOB_MERGED` is its own event type** — a merge is not a deletion, and the timeline should say so.

## Candidates raised, not ruled
Named in the 2026-09-02 assessment for later rulings; each one line, with what it buys:
- **"Agreed on the spot"** switch in Add job, creating the Job authorized in person — speed for walk-ins.
- **"Accept all complete"** at the end of an inspection — one tap instead of one per Finding.
- **Catalog suggestion** when the accepted Finding is a checklist item (brakes → เปลี่ยนผ้าเบรกหน้า) — a price-locked service line in two taps.
- **Per-shop "QC sign-off required"** setting; off turns Send to QC into Mark completed — no queue behind an absent Manager.
- **A Stage for approved, unstarted work** ("To start") — today such a case files under In assessment, so the board cannot answer "what can we start today?".
- **"Tell the customer"** after Start work, QC pass and Ready, opening the composer prefilled — prompt updates without an auto-send (ADR-003 intact).

## Done when
Fresh clone → migrate → seed → `npm run cases:stage` → log in. **At the car:** open a staged In-assessment case's inspection, accept a hood dent → the case page's Offer already holds "Hood — repaint", unpriced; accept two left-door findings, merge them in the Offer → one "Left-side repaint" line holding both Findings; mark brakes Needs work → Replace → Accept → a "Brakes — replace" line. Reopen the hood Finding → allowed; type a price on its line → the Finding is now frozen and the inspection screen says so; delete the line → the Finding reopens. **Price:** type prices into the cells; a catalog line is locked for the Advisor and live for the Manager. **Send:** Send quotation → the dialog shows three lines and the total → Send → Q-10xx v1 exists, the Customer Timeline shows the update with the label, the fake transport's outbox holds the Thai message with a link, and that link opens the light document without a session. Change one price, Send again → v2; send again unchanged → still v2. **Response:** the header's primary is Record response; the dialog records LINE · Q-10xx-v2 · yes, yes, no in one save → two lines in Work, one in Done, one authorization entry per Job, the case still Awaiting nothing. **Work:** the Authorized card leads with Start work; assign a technician in place; Start work → In progress leads with Send to QC; Waiting… → the reason dialog → the card leads with Resume work and the parts table sits under the blocker line; Send to QC → the Manager sees QC pass primary, the Advisor a hint; QC fail → required note → back to In progress; QC pass → Completed receipt; Cancel job lives only in ⋯ and asks for its note. **Done:** the declined line reads date · channel · note, and its revert is Manager-only in ⋯. **Delivered:** a delivered case's section is Done rows only, no heading, no controls. Both languages read naturally; every phase heading appears only when two or more phases have members; the header strip's actions open the dialogs. `case-flow`, the new jobs suite, `line-transport`, and `tenant-guard` pass.
