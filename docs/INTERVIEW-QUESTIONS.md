# Garage Interview Questions

The ⚠ flags in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) were converted to working assumptions on 2026-08-19 so the MVP build can start. This list validates — and may correct — those assumptions at the pilot shop and prospect garages; it no longer blocks anything.

## Shape & volume
- How many cars per week? Mix of body/paint vs. mechanical service vs. both on one visit?
- Typical number of distinct jobs per car? How long do cars sit at the shop (repair time vs. waiting time)?

## People & devices
- Who works here — roles and headcount (owner, advisor/front desk, body techs, painter, mechanic, parts/admin)?
- Who talks to customers today? Who would actually operate software?
- What devices exist — office PC, tablet, staff smartphones?

## Today's workflow
- Walk through yesterday's last check-in: how was it recorded (paper, whiteboard, LINE, Excel)?
- How are estimates built and communicated today? How is customer approval captured — and has "the customer never agreed to this" ever happened?
- What goes wrong most: lost photos, forgotten follow-ups, status questions by phone, missed parts?

## Insurance (validate the Claim model)
- What share of work is insurance-paid? Which insurers? In-network (อู่ในเครือ) or not?
- Walk through the last insurance case end-to-end: claim document (ใบเคลม), surveyor visit, who approved the repair scope, how hidden-damage supplements were approved, when and how the insurer actually paid.
- Who collects the deductible/excess, and when?

## QC (validate the QC gate)
- Is there a real check before customer pickup? Who does it?
- Would that person formally sign off in an app? What happens today when paint doesn't match?

## Parts
- Who orders parts, from where, and what's a typical ETA? How often do jobs stall waiting for parts, and how do you track "arrived"?

## Waiting & outsourcing
- What do jobs actually wait on — paint booth, technician availability, insurer answers, parts?
- Is any work outsourced (alignment, glass, upholstery)? *(If yes: the model may need an "outsourced/sublet" waiting reason or job flavor.)*

## Photos
- Do techs already share photos in a shop LINE group? Whose phones? Would they shoot before/after per job if asked?

## Money
- Are deposits taken? Partial payments? Cars released with balance due — and who chases it?
- How often do customers demand a tax invoice (ใบกำกับภาษี)? Who produces it today?

## LINE
- Does the shop have a LINE Official Account already, or only staff personal LINE? Friends count? Who mans the inbox? Would the owner pay LINE's message fees?

## CRM appetite
- Do you follow up declined work or due maintenance today? From memory, notebook, or not at all?
- What would make you actually switch from the current method — and what would make staff refuse to?
